## Everything is an operation

Every change (a drag, a Say result, a rename) is recorded as a **typed operation with an exact inverse**. That's what makes undo trustworthy: undo is not "restore a screenshot", it's "run the inverse".

The `core/` Rust crate defines the operations (`set`, `token`, `text`, `add`, `remove`, plus `insert`/`replace`) and has property-based tests: apply any random sequence of ops, then their inverses, and the tree comes back **exactly**.

## The op log

The app keeps an **op log** with groups (a Say answer with 12 ops is *one* undo step), forks and replay. It's saved in:

- the local design save,
- the exported project file (an optional `log` key; a damaged log is dropped, not fatal),
- the helper's durable backup.

Reload the page, restore from the helper, open a project file: your undo history comes with you. (e2e verifies the undo count survives all three.)

## Compare with original

**Frame ▾ → Compare with original** puts the selected take or copy next to the frame it came from, both filling the view.

## Merging: the maths is ready

Core has a **3-way merge** with per-property last-writer-wins: edits to *different* properties merge cleanly, same-property clashes are conflicts. It's deterministic, idempotent and commutes for disjoint edits (proptest-verified).
